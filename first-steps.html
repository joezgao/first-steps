First steps

A small agent starts out knowing nothing. All it is ever told is how it did: a little reward for getting closer to the light, a bonus for reaching it, a penalty for falling into the pit. After every 32 tries it nudges its network toward the moves that turned out better than it expected. Slide through its training and watch clumsy turn into skilled. Every creature here is the real policy, running live.
